## About Me

![jinghan](\images\jinghan1.jpeg)<br><br>My name is **Jinghan Lin（林婧涵)**, pronounced as [ʨiᵊŋ⁵¹ xan³⁵ lin³⁵]. <br><br>I am currently pursuing an M.A. in *Chinese Linguistics* at Peking University, under the supervision of [Prof. Yunjia Wang](https://chinese.pku.edu.cn/szdw/zzjs/1f2c9fb9e5674e4f879bc7a18d90e1db.htm). <br>My research interests lie in phonetics and phonology, particularly in acoustic and perceptual studies of sound variation and change, and cue weighting in sound contrasts.
